Mettlewake Studio is the private production tool behind the Mettlewake YouTube channel, which publishes animated short stories, motivation Shorts and ghost stories.

The tool is used only by the channel owner to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, manage playlists, and read the channel's own analytics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
