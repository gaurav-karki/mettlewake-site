Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Mettlewake Studio ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Mettlewake YouTube channel. It is used only by that owner, on their own Google account.

Data the app accesses

Through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, with the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own channel: video details (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists, privacy status), uploads made by the owner, and the channel's aggregated analytics (views, watch time, likes, comments, subscriber counts).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and manage the owner's videos and to record the channel's performance over time for the owner's own review.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored privately by the owner and are never published. Analytics are stored in a private database accessible only to the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data about viewers or any other Google users.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted on request.
